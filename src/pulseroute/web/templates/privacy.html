Privacy Policy & KVKK / GDPR Notice

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data Privacy Principles

PulseRoute is designed with Privacy-by-Design and Privacy-by-Default principles in accordance with EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Turkey Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK No. 6698).

2. IP Address Anonymization

We do not store raw visitor IP addresses in our databases. All incoming IP addresses are anonymized in memory prior to storage by truncating the final octet (IPv4 /24 mask) or prefixing (IPv6 /48 mask).

3. Cookieless Tracking

Our link redirection service does not write persistent tracking cookies on end-user browsers. Aggregate statistics (device type, country, browser family) are derived from User-Agent headers.

4. Data Encryption & Security

All sensitive credentials, API keys, and webhook secrets are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption. Database access is strictly parameterized to prevent injection vulnerabilities.
