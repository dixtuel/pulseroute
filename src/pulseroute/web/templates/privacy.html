Privacy Policy & Data Notice

Honest, user-centric explanation of what data is processed, how IP addresses are masked, and how your privacy is protected.

1. Scope & Data Controller

PulseRoute is open-source link-shortening software built with privacy as a foundational principle. This document outlines data handling at both the application and infrastructure level. The operator of this instance acts as the data controller under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK No. 6698) and the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

2. Privacy for Short Link Visitors (Cookieless & Masked IPs)

When you click on a PulseRoute short link, our highest priority is protecting your identity:

  • No Raw IP Storage: Your full IP address is never committed to permanent storage or databases. Incoming addresses are immediately anonymized by truncating the final octet for IPv4 (subnet /24, e.g. 198.51.100.45 becomes 198.51.100.0) or trailing bits for IPv6 (/48).
  • Zero Tracking Cookies on Redirects: The link redirection engine sets absolutely no tracking cookies, behavioral beacons, or persistent advertising fingerprints on your browser.
  • Aggregated Click Analytics: We record solely high-level telemetry (approximate country and city derived in-memory from the masked IP, device type, and referring website) to display aggregate visit graphs to the link creator.

3. Account Holders & Workspace Isolation

For users who choose to register an account:

  • Account Credentials: We store your email address and optional display name. Passwords are salted and hashed with bcrypt (one-way encryption). Plaintext passwords are never stored and cannot be viewed or decrypted by anyone, including the instance operator.
  • Strict Workspace Isolation: Every account is assigned an isolated workspace. Your shortened links, custom domain DNS records, and analytics streams are never accessible to other accounts.
  • Anonymous Links: Links created without creating an account expire automatically after 24 hours (TTL) and are permanently purged without retaining owner records.

4. Advertising & Cookie Consent (Google AdSense)

This instance utilizes Google AdSense to support server hosting expenses:

  • Ad units may appear on the bottom of the management console and on optional countdown interstitial redirect pages.
  • Consent-Gated Cookies: Personalized ad cookies and third-party advertising scripts are never loaded unless you grant explicit consent through the on-screen cookie notice. If you decline or ignore the banner, only non-personalized, privacy-safe advertisements (or none) will be served.
  • Your consent preferences (pr_pref) are stored locally in an encrypted, minimal format.

5. Security Measures & Field Encryption

PulseRoute is engineered according to modern security best practices:

  • SQL Injection Prevention: All database operations execute through parameterized asynchronous ORM queries (SQLAlchemy).
  • Brute-Force Lockout: Repeated failed login attempts trigger an automated 10-minute IP block to prevent credential stuffing.
  • Webhook Encryption: Sensitive integration tokens and webhook secrets are encrypted at rest using Fernet (AES-128-CBC + HMAC).

6. Your Rights & Data Erasure (KVKK & GDPR)

Under KVKK (Article 11) and GDPR (Articles 12–23), you hold the right to access, rectify, or request the complete erasure of your personal data.

When you request account deletion, your entire workspace—including all shortened links, tags, custom domain associations, and corresponding click statistics—is permanently and irreversibly deleted from the database. To exercise your rights, please reach out via the operator contact above.
