Terms of service

Clear standards for creating short links, protecting workspaces, and reporting harmful destinations.

ACCEPTABLE USE

01Nature of the platform

PulseRoute is an open-source link-shortening and redirection platform distributed under the MIT License. These terms govern your interaction with this specific running instance. If you self-host PulseRoute, you are the independent operator of your instance and set its policies.

02Acceptable use

Do not use this service to shorten or distribute:

  • Phishing sites, credential harvesters, fake banking portals, or deceptive sign-in pages.
  • Malware, spyware, ransomware, trojans, or hazardous executable files such as .exe, .scr, .bat, .vbs, .iso, or .ps1.
  • Internal network probes, loopback destinations, or cloud metadata services (SSRF).
  • Recursive redirect chains or attempts to mask the shortener domain to evade threat detection.
  • Scams, fraudulent crypto schemes, unlawful counterfeit material, spam campaigns, botnets, or mass unsolicited messages.

Destination URLs for desktop, iOS, Android, regional routing, and expiry fallbacks are screened when links are created or updated. Known malicious destinations, restricted file formats, and internal IP ranges are blocked.

03Accounts and workspaces

  • Each registered user receives an isolated workspace. Links, custom domains, tags, and click analytics are available only to members of that workspace.
  • You are responsible for the destinations and content linked through your workspace.
  • Anonymous links expire automatically after 24 hours. After expiry, they are removed and cannot be claimed, edited, or recovered.

04Rate limits and abuse response

Sliding-window rate limits protect the service from automated abuse. Repeated failed sign-ins trigger a 10-minute IP lockout. Abuse reports about phishing or malware can quarantine a link immediately. Other report types are counted once per reporter signal during the fixed 24-hour deduplication window; 5 distinct reports can quarantine a link and 10 can disable it. A disabled link record remains available for moderation and an owner appeal; it is not immediately erased from the database.

Reports submitted through the form are recorded in the service database; the form does not email the operator. Human review or a response within a specific timeframe is not guaranteed. Automated actions may be taken without prior human review. Accounts and workspaces found to repeatedly violate these terms may be closed.

This software is provided on an “AS IS” basis, without warranty of any kind, express or implied. The instance operator is not liable for user-provided destination content, temporary network interruptions, or third-party outages. See the Privacy Notice for information about data handling.

The online form records a report in the service database and does not email the operator. This service does not promise a human review, an immediate response, or a particular outcome for every report. Automated risk signals may block or quarantine a link without prior human review; they can miss harmful material or flag a link incorrectly.
