Terms of Service & Acceptable Use

Clear standards for short link creation, workspace security, and zero tolerance for abuse.

1. Nature of the Platform

PulseRoute is an open-source, privacy-respecting link shortening and redirection platform distributed under the MIT License. These terms govern your interaction with this specific running instance. If you self-host PulseRoute, you are the independent operator of your own instance and determine your own policies.

2. Acceptable Use Policy (Zero Tolerance for Abuse)

To keep short links safe, reliable, and trustworthy for all internet users, you agree never to use this service to shorten or distribute:

  • Phishing sites, credential harvesters, fake banking portals, or deceptive authentication pages,
  • Malware, spyware, ransomware, trojans, or hazardous executable files (e.g., .exe, .scr, .bat, .vbs, .iso, .ps1),
  • Internal network probes, loopback destinations, or cloud metadata services (Server-Side Request Forgery / SSRF),
  • Recursive redirect chains or masking the shortener domain to evade threat detection,
  • Scams, fraudulent crypto schemes, or unlawful counterfeit material,
  • Spam campaigns, botnets, or mass unsolicited messaging.

Every destination URL across all devices (desktop, iOS, Android, and regional geo-targets) is screened at creation and update time against known malicious domain registries, restricted file formats, and internal IP ranges. Links violating this policy are placed in immediate automated quarantine without prior notice.

3. Accounts & Workspace Isolation

Each registered user is assigned an isolated workspace upon sign-up:

  • Your links, custom domains, tags, and click analytics are accessible strictly to members of your workspace.
  • You are solely responsible for all destinations, redirects, and content linked through your workspace.
  • Anonymous Links: Links created without an account are assigned an automatic 24-hour lifespan (TTL). Once expired, they are purged and cannot be claimed, edited, or recovered.

4. Rate Limiting & Abuse Defense

To protect service availability and prevent automated abuse, all endpoints employ automated sliding-window rate limiting. Repeated failed login attempts trigger an automatic 10-minute IP lockout. Scraping or abusive bot traffic targeting link redirection is throttled at the network and application layers.

5. Warranty & Limitation of Liability

This software is provided on an "AS IS" basis, without warranty of any kind, express or implied. The instance operator is not liable for destination content provided by users, temporary network interruptions, or third-party outages. Users are advised to review the Privacy Policy to understand data handling practices.
